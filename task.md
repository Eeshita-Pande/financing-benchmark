# Northstar Compute: operating and financing plan

You are the analyst supporting the investment committee of Northstar Compute, a GPU infrastructure operator. The committee is considering the attached customer arrangements and hardware financing. It wants to understand the proposed economics, whether the business can meet its obligations and what the sponsor earns.

## 1. Assignment

Build an editable Excel model for January 2028 through December 2031 using the supplied source records and agreements. Show monthly revenue, operating profit, collections, customer balances, debt service, covenant compliance, cash and additional funding requirements. Calculate the sponsor's annualised return using the specified cash-flow dates.

Assess the indicative commercial proposal and present an implementable recommendation under the signed terms. The lender's financial constraints must be respected wherever the agreements permit. The sponsor prefers to avoid additional support where possible. Identify any case that cannot meet its obligations with committed resources, quantify the shortfall and explain the decisions needed.

Prices, service obligations, equipment costs and the signed financing commitments are fixed within each supplied case. Do not assume an unagreed waiver, additional borrowing, revised customer prices or unlimited equity. The committee may subsequently revise the supplied business assumptions, so the workbook must remain usable when those inputs change.

## 2. Materials and authority

- `before.xlsx`: numerical terms, opening balances, case definitions and source records. It contains no financial model.
- `contracts.md`: customer agreements and amendments.
- `funding.md`: facility, sponsor support and return conventions.
- `accounting-and-operations.md`: the accounting policy for this exercise, costs and opening balances.

The workbook governs numerical facts. The documents govern contractual meaning and accounting treatment. All amounts are nominal USD; there is no FX, income tax, VAT, bad-debt expense or inflation unless an input expressly states otherwise. All names and amounts are fictional.

## 3. Required cases and deliverables

Evaluate all four cases in the source workbook. The delayed-receipt case becomes known after the affected month's anchor invoice has been issued. The operating-incident case changes the stated expense and its payment, not customer demand or contractual revenue. The final case uses the alternate renewal clause supplied in the agreement.

Return `model.xlsx` and a short `recommendation.md` explaining the material results, changes from the indicative proposal and any remaining obligations or financing limitations. Include enough supporting work for an investment committee analyst to review the model. No particular worksheet names, cell addresses or software library are required.
