# Hardware financing and sponsor terms

## 1. Acquisition and sources

New hardware costs the amount in Terms, paid on signing. The lender advances 70% of that cost and the sponsor contributes the other 30%, both on 1 January 2028. These amounts are independent of customer advances. The loan is fully drawn on that date. There is no revolver, additional tranche, refinancing or optional principal prepayment. Customer advances are unrestricted operating cash but are excluded from the covenant numerator at receipt.

## 2. Debt service

Interest is paid monthly at the annual rate divided by twelve, on opening principal plus any first-day draw. Equal principal instalments begin in the first principal-payment month and continue for the stated number of months. Principal never exceeds debt outstanding. Payments occur at month-end. The agent fee is paid at each calendar quarter-end while principal is outstanding immediately before that month's repayment, including the final repayment month if it is a quarter-end.

The contractual debt-service denominator is scheduled principal plus cash interest plus the cash agent fee. No interest capitalisation or amortisation holiday is available beyond the stated principal commencement.

## 3. Covenant

The minimum monthly cash DSCR is the multiple in Terms. Testing begins in the first covenant-test month and applies in each month with debt service thereafter. The ratio is not tested during the two commissioning months. Months with no debt service have no DSCR denominator.

Cash available for debt service comprises current cash receipts from service invoices, after invoice credits, less operating cash payments and customer cash refunds. It excludes signing advances, loan proceeds, hardware sponsor equity, opening cash, depreciation and other non-cash items. Receipts from opening receivables qualify when collected; payments of opening operating payables are deducted when paid. Capex is outside this numerator.

Actual receipts govern the test. Cash already accumulated on the balance sheet does not become covenant income merely because it can fund debt service. Contractual payment dates remain relevant to the commercial proposal; a subsequently disclosed delay does not alter an already final invoice.

## 4. Committed support and remaining shortfalls

The sponsor has a separate support commitment with both the monthly cap and lifetime cap in Terms. At month-end it funds the larger of the amount required to restore the covenant and the amount required to restore the minimum unrestricted cash balance, limited by both available caps. A dollar funded can satisfy both requirements; it is not contributed twice. This support is additional equity, not customer revenue or borrowing.

The lender permits the portion needed for the current month's covenant shortfall to be included as a current-month equity cure. It cannot raise covenant credit above the shortfall or create capacity for additional customer concessions. Show the operating ratio and the ratio after the permitted cure distinctly. Support used only for liquidity is not a covenant cure.

If committed support is insufficient, the covenant breach and/or funding shortfall remains unresolved. Do not assume a waiver or change the financing terms. For this exercise, continue the contractual forecast without acceleration, default interest, service cessation or cancellation: the committee needs the size and timing of the unresolved obligations. A negative cash path is an unfunded counterfactual, not an authorised bank overdraft.

## 5. Sponsor return convention

At the forecast start, the sponsor acquires the existing equity for the opening acquisition value in Terms and separately contributes the hardware equity. The acquisition payment goes to the selling shareholder and is not cash received by the operator. Subsequent support is paid on the last calendar day of its funding month.

The board retains all operating cash during the forecast. There are no interim distributions. On 31 December 2031, all remaining cash is distributed after customer and supplier obligations are settled, and the minimum-cash retention requirement ceases. No equipment sale proceeds, terminal enterprise value or further recovery are assumed. The return is annualised using actual days on a 365-day basis from the specified cash-flow dates.

Do not present an unfunded or covenant-defaulting plan as an achievable sponsor return. Identify its financing limitations separately. Retention of cash is a fixed board policy in all cases, not a modelling choice.
