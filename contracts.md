# Customer agreements

Forecast month 1 is January 2028. “Service month” counts from a customer's service commencement. All calculations use unrounded amounts; invoices are evaluated at monthly aggregate precision. The case uses whole-month payment lags and ignores weekends. A payment with a one-month lag is received on the last calendar day of the following month. Opening balances have their own payment dates.

## A1. Anchor customer: Helix Research

The contract is signed and new hardware funded on 1 January 2028. Stand-ready service commences in forecast month 3 for 36 months, at the monthly fixed charge in Terms. The fixed charge does not depend on utilisation. The supplied metered hours determine power expense only. No service is provided before commencement.

The operator invoices on the first day of each service month and the customer pays the balance on the last day of that month. Payment is punctual in every supplied case. Invoice credits are final when issued. Contract consideration is the sum of the fixed service charges; an advance is part of that consideration.

## A2. Advance and indicative commercial exhibit

The customer pays the stated percentage of the initial term's fixed consideration on signing. Renewal consideration is outside that percentage base. The operator must provide dollar-for-dollar invoice credit or return the unapplied amount as cash under the agreement. Credits cannot exceed an invoice's gross charge or the advance still outstanding. There is no discount or interest on the customer balance.

The executed agreement specifies the total credit entitlement and its final settlement date. It imposes no monthly minimum application, no fixed intermediate vesting dates and no charge for changing the designation of an unissued invoice. Already issued invoices cannot be amended retrospectively.

The commercial team's indicative exhibit has zero credit weight in the first 24 service months and equal positive weights in the remaining initial-term months. The advance is allocated in proportion to those weights. The exhibit is a nonbinding proposal, not an additional contractual obligation. Any balance present on commencement of a permitted renewal has equal indicative weights across its remaining months.

## A3. Anchor renewal and final settlement

The six-month renewal follows the initial term without a gap, at the lower fixed monthly charge in Terms. Initial-term invoices and customer balances remain enforceable; they are not cancelled or reissued by the renewal. There is no new advance.

Where the case's renewal transfer field is 1, the existing advance remains usable on renewal invoices and its final settlement date moves to the renewal expiry. Where that field is 0, the renewal has no right to use the old advance and the old settlement date stays at the initial-term expiry. All other renewal terms remain the same.

Any unapplied advance is repayable in cash on the last day of the month immediately following its applicable settlement expiry. It cannot be retained as income, silently extended, used against another customer's invoice or offset against an unrelated obligation.

## B. Usage customer: Vector Labs

Usage is measured in GPU-hours for each calendar month and billed at the price in Terms. An invoice is issued on the fifth day of the following month. Its contractual collection month is the service month plus the whole-month lag in Terms; the standard lag is one month. Changing that lag does not change the issue date or service period. Supported lag revisions are one to three months.

The delayed-receipt case postpones payment of the invoice for service month 24 by two additional months beyond its contractual collection month. The delay is disclosed immediately after the anchor invoice for the original collection month has become final. There is no default, write-off, penalty or service suspension. Only that invoice is late; later invoices keep their original dates. The opening receivable is separate from forecast service and keeps its stated payment date.

## C1. Minimum-commitment customer: Mosaic AI

Each month through the final service month, the customer owes at least the monthly minimum in Terms. Metered service is valued using the applicable contractual price. The customer is billed at month-end for the minimum or, when higher, the value of service less valid make-up credits used against that month's overage. Payment follows the collection lag in Terms.

## C2. Make-up entitlements

When service value is below the minimum, the difference becomes a dollar-denominated make-up entitlement. It may be used against service above a later month's minimum, starting in the month after issue and ending at the close of the final month of its stated validity window. It cannot offset the minimum itself. Entitlements are non-refundable and have no cash or interest value.

Apply the earliest-expiring eligible entitlement first, and the oldest issue first when expiry dates tie. Service in an expiry month can use the entitlement before it expires at month-end. The final service month is the outer limit on every entitlement's validity, including entitlements created that month. Credit issue, application and expiry are separate events.

## C3. Minimum-customer amendment

The amendment becomes effective in forecast month 39. It changes the usage price and monthly minimum to the amended values in Terms; the final service month remains month 44. Previously issued invoices keep their amounts and payment dates. Existing make-up entitlements retain their dollar face value and original expiry dates. They can be used against eligible overage under the amended contract. No entitlement balance is reset and there is no retroactive repricing.
